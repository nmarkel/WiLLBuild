## Staging Rule

Inventory `assembly-part` products that are **not** among the 15 curated wizard parts are stored with `slot: "standalone"` and `tier: 3`. They cannot join the 3D wizard until Task 6 provides:

1. Socket definitions (`sockets`, `mount`)
2. A parametric placeholder spec (`placeholder`)
3. A slot flip to the correct wizard slot (`fixture` / `arm` / `pole` / `baseCover`)

To find upgrade candidates: `catalog.parts.filter(p => p.productClass === "assembly-part" && p.slot === "standalone")`
